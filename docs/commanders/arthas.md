# Arthas — The Scourge

**Role:** Beginner-friendly large army, sustained fights  
**Secondary resource:** Souls (from enemy kills)

## Army (MVP)

| Unlock | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Lv 1 | Necropolis, Acolytes, Ghouls, Death Knight hero |
| Lv 2 | Necromancers (starting escort) |
| Lv 3 | Death Coil mastery *(design stub — hero ability focus)* |
| Lv 4 | Halls of the Dead *(design stub)* |
| Lv 5 | **Raise Dead** (`-raise`) — spend 8 Souls for Ghoul + skeletons |
| Lv 6+ | Abominations / stubs |
| Lv 10 | **Army of the Damned** (`-army`) — spend 50 Souls for mass raise |

## Signature mechanic

Fallen enemies have a chance to yield free skeletons once Raise Dead is unlocked. Souls fuel commander abilities.

## Chat commands

- `-raise` — special ability  
- `-army` — ultimate  
- `-status` — resource / level
