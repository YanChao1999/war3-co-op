# Thrall — The Horde

**Role:** Aggressive, mobile army with temporary summons  
**Secondary resource:** Spirit Energy (from enemy kills)

## Army (MVP)

| Unlock | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Lv 1 | Great Hall, Peons, Grunts, Thrall hero |
| Lv 2 | Raiders (starting escort) |
| Lv 3 | Chain Lightning mastery *(design stub)* |
| Lv 4 | Spirit Lodge *(design stub)* |
| Lv 5 | **Call Elemental** (`-elemental`) — spend 10 Spirit for a timed water elemental |
| Lv 6+ | Tauren on ultimate / stubs |
| Lv 10 | **Elemental Storm** (`-storm`) — spend 50 Spirit for multiple timed elementals |

## Signature mechanic

Temporary elemental summons (timed life) create burst power without permanent supply bloat.

## Chat commands

- `-elemental` — special ability  
- `-storm` — ultimate  
- `-status` — resource / level  

## Prestige (future)

Stormcaller vs Warchief playstyles — not in MVP.
